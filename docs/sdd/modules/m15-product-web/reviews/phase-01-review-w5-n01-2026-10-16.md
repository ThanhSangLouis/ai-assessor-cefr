## Findings

### `M15-W5-N01-P01` — MAJOR · Requirement scope and traceability

The M15 draft covers all four UI work packages, while the requested deliverable is W5-N01. The active task needs an explicit trace from N01.1–N01.7 to applicable FR/AC/Test IDs and a clear out-of-scope boundary for N02–N04.

**Required resolution:** revise the Requirement/traceability for W5-N01 without claiming teacher, learning, or admin flows are included; identify the contract-dependent student evidence blocks and packaging handoff.

### `M15-W5-N01-P02` — MAJOR · Observable behavior and acceptance

Several acceptance criteria use terms such as “đạt”, “đúng”, “mọi page chính”, and “không vượt evidence” without identifying the routes, state, expected recovery, or a measurable accessibility target.

**Required resolution:** specify the N01 route/role matrix, critical journey states, denied/error/retry outcomes, no-false-zero/no-forbidden-claim rules, desktop/mobile matrix, and the accessibility target selected by the project owner.

### `M15-W5-N01-P03` — BLOCKER · Research and producer contracts

The Requirement proposes Research `RUN` but no user choice is recorded. M15 Tasks 001/002 are `BLOCKED` in this baseline; week-05 dependencies include producer contracts and owner review for identity/consent, upload/QC, trust/report, learning evidence, and asset packaging.

**Required resolution:** owner selects `RUN` or `SKIP`; relevant module owners provide approved contract revisions before Phase 05 readiness. Existing or mocked UI behavior alone cannot satisfy W5-N01 Done.
